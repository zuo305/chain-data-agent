# Review: 2026-11-21 (both projects)

Covers chain-data-agent since launch (2026-11-14, 8 days) and
agent-data-platform (NSW planning data) over the last 30 days
(2026-10-22 to 2026-11-21). Sources: `/admin/usage` of each Worker, D1
`usage_log`, USDC transfers to the shared payee wallet on Base, and the
CDP discovery API. Bot traffic is the logged weight (bots are sampled 1 in
10). Earlier NSW review: agent-data-platform
`notes/reports/2026-11-07-week-review.md`.

## Summary

Both services are widely discovered and work end to end, but in 38 days
(NSW) and 8 days (chain data) no agent has paid for the data, used a free
call for its own purposes, or sent a real data request. Every payment came
from a directory, verifier, sampler or test harness. Discovery is not the
gap: there is no buyer yet.

## Who pays

Every payer wallet pays many unrelated x402 services, which is what
verifiers and samplers do (details and wallet list in CLAUDE.md):

- Directory reviews: PayAPI Market (one call per newly submitted route),
  nohumans.directory, Market402.
- Trust and delivery checkers: SettledScout (pays each service once,
  ~200 a day), LumiTrustBuyer, agents-trust, vet402.
- Samplers: `0xaa34…1db3` (20-28 services every 6 hours), `0x08ed…d5f7`
  (one new service every ~12 hours since 2026-11-06, never repeats),
  `0x82ce…6f86` (~2,700 payments to ~860 services a day).
- Test harness: usetested.com (10 calls in 40 s on 2026-11-21; ~600
  payments to ~50 services a day).

The useful side effect: payments settle reliably (including 10 in a row),
and the settlements are what got our routes listed in the CDP Bazaar
without paying ourselves.

## Why there is no demand (assessment)

1. **The x402 economy's buyers are mostly measurers.** Hundreds of distinct
   bots crawl, rate and sample paid services; we found no wallet that buys
   the same kind of data repeatedly for its own task. Supply of paid
   endpoints is far ahead of agents holding funded wallets.
2. **Price is not the barrier.** NSW offers 3 free MCP calls per client per
   day and in 30 days one client used one. People are not adding the MCP
   server to the agents they use (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex), so they never
   reach the free calls, let alone payment.
3. **Directory listings reach bots, not people.** About 20 listings
   (MCP Registry, Glama, Smithery, CDP Bazaar, 402 Index, PayAPI,
   agent-tools.cloud, mcpservers.org, ...) produced tens of thousands of
   probe requests and about 16 search or social referrals in total.
4. **Both datasets are niche.** NSW planning data matters to property
   people in one state; SOL lockups and validator stats matter to Solana
   stakers and funds. Neither audience browses x402 directories.

## Options

- **A. Keep both running in low-maintenance mode.** They cost $0 on free
  tiers and stay listed, so any demand that appears is still captured.
  Only fix breakage; no new datasets.
- **B. Demand-side research before building anything else.** Use the same
  on-chain method on the whole x402 market: find services on Base that
  receive repeat payments from wallets that are not samplers or verifiers,
  and see what those buyers want. This answers "what do agents actually
  pay for" with data instead of guesses.
- **C. One channel experiment per project, aimed at people.** NSW: the
  planned Apify Actor (a marketplace whose buyers already pay per run) or
  a post in a proptech or developer community with a concrete use case.
  Chain data: Solana staking communities, with the free summary
  endpoints as the hook.
- **D. Stop.** Archive the repos and unlist. Saves attention, not money,
  since running costs are already zero.

## Recommendation

A + B now, C for one project only if B finds nothing better:

1. Freeze feature work on both projects; keep them up and listed.
2. Spend the next session on the x402 demand research (B): repeat buyers,
   what they buy, at what price.
3. Set a stop rule: if by 2026-12-21 neither project has one payment or
   free call from a client that is not a verifier, stop active work on it
   and keep it running unattended.

## Housekeeping found during the review

- A `HEAD` request to a paid route answers 200 with no body (Hono runs the
  GET handler for HEAD; the payment layer only matches GET). No data
  leaks, but it shows up as a 200 in the stats and costs a handler run.
- Roll the Cloudflare API token that was pasted into a chat on 2026-11-14;
  `/admin/usage` now works through the network secret, so the token is no
  longer needed for usage checks.
